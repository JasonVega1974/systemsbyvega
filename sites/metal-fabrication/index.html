Straight answers

Asked at every job site

Do you take small repair jobs?+
Yes. A cracked hitch or a broken gate hinge is a real job to me, not a nuisance. Small repairs usually slot in between bigger builds within a few days — text a photo and I'll tell you what it'll run.
How far will the mobile rig travel?+
Anywhere in the Treasure Valley — Caldwell, Nampa, Middleton, Star, Boise and the farm ground between. Farther out is usually fine too with a small trip charge; text your location and I'll confirm before you commit to anything.
How do quotes work?+
Simple: we talk through the job, I put one fixed number in writing, and that number is what you pay. Material price swings are my problem to manage, not a surprise on your invoice. Quotes are free.
Can I get it powder coated?+
Yes. I prep and partner with a local powder coater for a factory-grade finish in any standard color — tougher and cleaner than rattle-can paint. It's quoted as a line item so you can take it or leave it.
What are lead times like?+
Repairs: usually inside a week. Custom builds: typically two to four weeks depending on the board. I'll give you a real date with the quote — and if the board shifts, you hear it from me before it becomes your problem.
I have an idea but no design. Can you help?+
Absolutely — that's most projects. Bring a photo from the internet, a napkin sketch, or just a rough idea, and I'll turn it into a dimensioned drawing you approve before anything gets cut.
