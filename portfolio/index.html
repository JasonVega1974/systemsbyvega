About

Jason Vega is a solo developer and founder in Idaho who takes production software from first sketch to deployed site — and ships every layer himself. By day he is Vice President of Digital Strategy & Innovation at Roady's Truck Stops, leading digital for a national truck-stop network since 2019; that operator's instinct shapes everything he builds on his own.

Through Systems by Vega he gets small businesses off spreadsheets and onto real systems — custom databases, operations dashboards, and process that holds up in daily use. Through Kingdom Creatives LLC he builds websites and digital tools for churches and families, keeping technology in service of people.

The work spans faith, commercial, and creative: YourLife CC, a family-and-faith PWA with its guided Faith Journey; The Return, a retreat inventory system; Legacy Vault, a secure home for family memories; Estate Sale Biz; and the Dom Vegz music-brand site — even this hub runs on an admin he wrote himself. He ships solo on a lean, modern stack: Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, and Brevo, with AI-assisted development in Claude Code and VS Code.
